About Us

Mangal Shri: Menswear & Groom Couture

Mangal Shri is a menswear showroom in Ibrahimpura, Bhopal, dedicated to dressing the modern groom. From handcrafted Jodhpuri bandhgalas and heritage sherwanis to indo-western sets, coat suits, blazers, kurta payjama and pure silk fabrics, everything is chosen and tailored for weddings and festive occasions.

Our multi-storey showroom lets you try outfits in person, and our master tailors take live measurements and make adjustments so every outfit fits you exactly. Can’t visit? Message us on WhatsApp for styling help, safa matching and video consultations.
